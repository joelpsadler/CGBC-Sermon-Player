## New OpenBible-derived geography

**Original source:** OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding Data, `https://github.com/openbibleinfo/Bible-Geocoding-Data`.
**License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, `https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`.
**Extraction/normalization:** `BSB-publishing/bsb-data-output`, `base/geography/places.jsonl`, blob SHA `4645c9dc2677c951daa71387461ab4598d10735f`.
**Changes:** 64 selective place records extracted, source identifiers and candidate modern-site coordinates retained, integrated with existing CGBC people/events and conservative geographic-display restrictions; unrelated raw location data and presentation material intentionally excluded. This is a derivative subset and is not endorsed by OpenBible.info, BSB Publishing, or its contributing scholars.

**Attribution:** Biblical geographic information © respective contributors. Place-identification data courtesy of OpenBible.info, CC BY 4.0, via BSB Publishing's normalized geographic extraction. The upstream work is not necessarily a consensus biblical atlas. Consult the cited original sources when high geographic precision matters. Confidence scores are source values, not percentages or proof of certainty.

The geographic data should remain separately attributable if incorporated into a publicly accessible map. Any further redistribution must carry this notice, CC BY 4.0 license link, and modification notice. No OpenStreetMap shape geometry or satellite/photographic images were imported; those source assets may carry distinct license conditions.

## Previously merged sources (unchanged inherited data)

- **Theonize Interactive Bible Timeline:** MIT, licensed source historical-events dataset and biblical/secular proposed dates. See `licenses/LICENSE_THEONIZE_MIT.txt`.
- **Daniel-Hug/bible-people:** MIT, source genealogy data and Scripture evidence. See `licenses/LICENSE_DANIEL_HUG_MIT.txt`.
- **Wazzac People of the Bible:** MIT for original project code and material; CGBC has labels and network candidate relationships, not BibleProject posters, image assets or restricted NIV quotations. See `licenses/LICENSE_WAZZAC_MIT.txt`.
- **Kenneth Reitz KJV Study:** ISC for project-authored portions. The legacy PAF/Gilliland GEDCOM inside that repository remains excluded because its redistribution status as an independent third-party artifact is not fully established. See `licenses/LICENSE_KJV_STUDY_ISC.txt`.
- **Theographic** (CC BY-SA), OpenStreetMap geometry (ODbL), proprietary GED-VIEW and third-party maps/images are **not** bulk-imported. A separate explicitly licensed overlay is needed before any such content is added.

Chronology date proposals are attributed research claims, not necessarily independent corroboration, and any direct excerpts from third-party writings require their own rights assessment. No NASB95 licensed verse text is reproduced by this library; NASB95 verse catalog and usage ledger are unaffected.

## New research dates

- The principal early (mid-15th century BC) and later (13th century BC) Exodus proposals are discussed in Biblical Archaeology Society's `https://library.biblicalarchaeology.org/sidebar/dating-the-exodus/` and James K. Hoffmeier, Journal of the Evangelical Theological Society 50:2 (2007), `https://www.galaxie.com/article/jets50-2-01`. Date labels from those sources are summarized independently, with the disagreement preserved.
- Matthew 20's teaching context derives from Matthew 19–20. Its proposed AD 30–33 date is a broad first-century Passion chronology model rather than a quoted biblical calendar date.
